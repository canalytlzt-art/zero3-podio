---
name: ZERO3 logical batteries
description: The user-approved identity and TV behavior for a competition battery.
---

A logical battery has a stable internal ID; event + displayed order is its unique, editable position, shown as P01, P02, and so on. Renaming or moving it must retain its ID, registrations, timer, results, publications, and TV selection. If a target position is occupied, require an explicit atomic swap. Deletion must not renumber other batteries and may remove only that battery's own operational data; preserve the event, registrations, global athletes, proofs, and other batteries, and clear TV selection if it pointed to the deleted battery. It contains event registrations and runs all four configured proofs in parallel; RX and SC may share a battery but remain separate in results and rankings. The shared event timer is independent of TV selection and battery finalization. Selecting a battery only changes what the TV displays. Finalization requires a reviewed result or configured official exception for every expected registration and proof, publishes the complete battery snapshot, and updates ranking. Repeating finalization without changes must not create a duplicate publication.

Overall proof points rank current results across finalized batteries of the same event, proof, and category. A changed result remains pending until the battery is reviewed and finalized again. Unresolved ties or exceptions do not earn zero points or make the event ranking final. When migrating legacy category/proof-specific heat rows, never merge rows with different registration sets automatically; compare their registrations and preserve the source rows and results until the user approves an explicit mapping.

**Why:** The user superseded the earlier proof-scoped model with event-scoped batteries containing four parallel proofs, while retaining separate RX/SC classifications. They require a stable battery ID across editable display positions, atomic explicit swaps, no renumbering after deletion, timer commands and TV selection to remain independent, review before merging legacy rows with different teams, and live results to stay distinct from official standings until publication.

**How to apply:** Use the stable battery ID for all links and event + displayed order for uniqueness and Pnn display. Attach each event registration at most once. Keep all four proof results under that battery; calculate official proof placements only from finalized snapshots and keep RX/SC rankings isolated. Public views may show open-battery results as provisional, but they must not change official points or podiums before publication. Do not infer finalization from timer state. Preserve timer and TV references across reordering; when positions conflict, swap in one transaction. Delete only the selected battery and its dependent operational rows, never renumber survivors. Before migrating legacy rows, compare registration sets; stop and request mapping approval when they differ. Preserve source IDs and results until that mapping is explicitly resolved.

When a result has no legacy heat or battery link, preserve it as pending and do not infer its battery from category or proof alone. Require an explicit mapping decision before including it in battery cells or rankings.

**Why:** The user chose to leave two validated RX results without battery links and pause the staging release rather than risk attributing them to the wrong battery.

**How to apply:** Migration diagnostics may report these rows, but must not create battery entries or change result links automatically. Pause publication until the mapping is explicitly resolved.
