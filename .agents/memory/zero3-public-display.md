---
name: ZERO3 public display
description: Product requirements for the ZERO3 PRO public competition TV.
---

The public display is one landscape TV page with a live Brasília civil clock, a separate sports duration, and simultaneous SC and RX panels. Confirmed results appear in their category without losing earlier heats, and manually published podiums stay in those same panels. Administrative operation remains separate.

**Why:** The user explicitly replaced the earlier split timer and leaderboard screens with one public TV page.

**How to apply:** Use this as the source of truth for future ZERO3 public-screen design and implementation. Preserve registrations, IDs, and prior results; use the explicit `America/Sao_Paulo` timezone; keep administrative actions off the TV.
